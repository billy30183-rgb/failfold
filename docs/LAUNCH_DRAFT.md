**Show HN: FailFold — Fold repeated JUnit failures locally without uploading logs**

I built FailFold to make repeated JUnit failures easier to read. Its synthetic demo folds 240 failure records into three signature groups while preserving every occurrence. Compare a supplied baseline and export Markdown or JSON, using a browser or Node CLI. Reports stay local. Matching signatures do not prove a shared root cause, and fewer reading entries are not measured debugging time saved. JUnit support covers a documented subset; exports may contain private information.

Try it: https://billy30183-rgb.github.io/failfold/
